Go-to-market plan

Selling six residences with Angela Webb

With only six residences, this is a precision campaign, not a volume one. The goal is a short list of qualified, finance-ready buyers who see plans and pricing privately before any public release. The concept is not yet consented, so every piece is labelled as an artist's impression and concept design. Figures marked "estimate" are starting assumptions to test.

Perfect customer

Primary

Central-city downsizers

Owners aged 55 to 75 in a large family home in Fendalton, Merivale, Strowan, St Albans, Cashmere or a lifestyle block, with significant equity and grown children.

  • Trigger: the garden and stairs are too much, travel plans, or wanting to walk to dinner and the park.
  • Wants: one level, a lift, three bedrooms for family visits, privacy, secure parking and a sunny balcony.
  • Objections: body-corporate living, giving up space, buying off the plans before consent.

Primary

Professionals and lock-and-leave owners

Couples aged 40 to 60, often medical, legal or business owners working in the central city, or Canterbury farmers and Queenstown or offshore owners wanting a city base.

  • Trigger: a short commute, a second home in town, or returning from overseas.
  • Wants: a whole floor, the residents' sauna, spa and gym, secure parking, rooftop solar and low maintenance.
  • Objections: resale depth for a new product type and timing.

Secondary

Returning expats

New Zealanders in Australia, the UK and Asia planning to come home, who know Christchurch's rebuilt centre by reputation.

  • Objections: buying sight unseen; answered with video walkthroughs and a call with Angela.

Secondary

Family buying for parents

Adult children helping a parent move closer to the hospital and the city, often deciding together.

  • Objections: accessibility and the lift; answered by the level-living Garden Residence and a lift to every floor.

Tracking

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API: a "Lead" event on successful registration, browser and server, deduplicated.
  • Google Ads conversion tag and GA4: a "generate_lead" event with residence and budget band as parameters.
  • UTM tags on every ad, so each registration shows its platform, angle and ad number.
  • Weekly report: spend, registrations, cost per registration, budget bands and the best ads.

Traditional media: billboards, print and outdoor

For six homes the traditional mix should be small and well placed: one or two prestige outdoor sites, a few quality print insertions and beautiful site hoarding. Every piece carries Angela, one call to action and the site address. The mock-ups below are flat artwork concepts in simple settings for discussion, not booked placements.

Booking a Christchurch billboard

Step 1Brief suppliers

Ask the main outdoor companies (such as JCDecaux, oOh!media and LUMO Digital Outdoor) for premium sites and rates.

Step 2Choose sites

Routes buyers use daily: Memorial Avenue, Fendalton Road, Papanui Road, Bealey Avenue and the central city.

Step 3Book dates

Digital boards book in 1 to 2 week slots; printed boards usually 4 weeks or more.

Step 4Supply artwork

The supplier's exact size. Seven words or fewer, plus Angela and the web address.

Step 5Track

Watch direct visits and searches for "The Rise Christchurch" during the run.

Rules for every ad

  • Label renders as artist's impressions and plans as concept designs subject to consent, in line with the Fair Trading Act.
  • No prices, completion dates or "luxury" claims that can't be backed. Areas are gross concept areas and say so.
  • Include Bayleys branding and the licence line wherever the format has room.
